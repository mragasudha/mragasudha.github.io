E-Guide: How to choose the right Continuous Testing partner

HCL OneTest Buyer education content DevSecOps audience Lead generation asset

A gated e-guide written to educate software engineering and QA leaders on continuous testing — and position HCL OneTest as the right partner for their DevSecOps journey. Written, structured, and produced by me as part of the OneTest demand generation program.

Gated lead gen asset DevSecOps / QA audience Technical buyer education Demand generation Product positioning

The brief

My goal was to create a piece of content that would do three things simultaneously: educate buyers who were still evaluating whether to invest in continuous testing at all, build credibility for HCL OneTest against more recognized vendors, and generate qualified leads by offering genuine value in exchange for contact information.

The audience was technical — software engineering leaders, QA managers, and DevOps practitioners — but the decision to download and share the guide would often involve business stakeholders too. The content had to work at both levels.

Strategic choices

The most important decision was not to lead with the product. A lot of vendor e-guides make the mistake of starting with "here's why our product is great." That's a fast way to lose a technical buyer who hasn't yet decided if they even need the category.

Instead, I structured the guide to meet buyers where they are in their journey:

  • Category education first — what is continuous testing, and why does it matter now? This earns trust with readers who are still exploring.
  • Vendor-neutral criteria — what should you look for in any CT solution and partner? This positions us as a credible guide, not a sales pitch.
  • Soft product introduction last — HCL OneTest is introduced only in the conclusion, after trust is established.

Content structure

The e-guide follows a deliberate narrative arc from problem awareness to solution confidence:

Table of contents

01Introduction — the quality vs. speed tension in modern software delivery
02What is continuous testing? — category education for evaluators
03Getting started — how CT fits into DevSecOps and your delivery pipeline
04Choosing the right solution — a vendor-neutral evaluation framework
05Choosing the right provider — what to look for beyond the technology
06Benefits — the business case for CT, framed for stakeholder buy-in
07Conclusion + HCL OneTest introduction

How it fit into the broader GTM

This e-guide was one asset in a multi-channel demand generation program for HCL OneTest. It sat at the top of the funnel — gated on the website to capture leads, promoted through LinkedIn and email campaigns to DevOps and QA audiences, and referenced in analyst briefings as evidence of thought leadership investment.

It was also used by the sales team as a pre-meeting send — giving prospects something substantive to read before a discovery call, which improved the quality of those conversations.

PMM skill demonstrated

Creating effective buyer education content requires the same skills as positioning: deep understanding of the buyer's job-to-be-done, the competitive landscape they're navigating, and the narrative arc that moves them from awareness to consideration.
