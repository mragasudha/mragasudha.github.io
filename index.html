About

I've spent 6+ years in B2B marketing, with the last 4+ focused on product marketing for developer-centric software. Most recently at Atlassian, where I owned product marketing for Atlassian Automation — and before that at HCL Software, where I owned product marketing for HCL OneTest, a cloud-native test automation platform.

What I enjoy most about this work is translating complex technical products into stories that actually resonate with the people buying them. That means understanding how technical buyers think, what they're skeptical of, and what genuinely moves them.

In 2023 I took time off for personal reasons. Since coming back I've completed two certifications in AI and marketing and have been experimenting with AI-powered workflows — a few of which you can see on this site.
