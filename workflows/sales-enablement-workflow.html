AI Workflow · Sales Enablement

Sales enablement content at scale

⏱ 1–3 hours per asset

Use this workflow to generate first drafts of battle cards, objection-handling guides, talk tracks, and one-pagers. Feed Claude your product context and competitive details upfront, get a structured draft, then format for sales with Gamma.

Claude ChatGPT Gamma

What this covers

Battle cards

Head-to-head competitor comparisons for sales reps in active deals

Objection-handling guides

Common objections mapped to responses with proof points

Talk tracks

Discovery and demo guides by persona or use case

One-pagers

Leave-behinds summarizing value prop for a specific segment

Step 1 · ~20 min

Battle card — brief Claude and generate

Prompt — battle card

I need a battle card comparing [our product] vs [Competitor]. - Our product: [brief description, key capabilities] - Our ICP: [role, company size, pain points] - Competitor: [their product and positioning] - Where we typically win: [2-3 reasons] - Where we typically lose: [1-2 honest weaknesses] - Most common objection: [specific objection] Produce a battle card with: 1. One-line summary of the competitive dynamic 2. Where we win — 4-5 advantages with a proof point each 3. Where they win — 2-3 areas (be honest) 4. Top 3 objections and a response to each 5. Landmines — things to avoid saying 6. Discovery questions to use when this competitor is in the deal Keep it scannable.

Sample output:

Claude's response — excerpt

Quick summary: We win on multi-cloud coverage and developer experience. They win on brand recognition and enterprise relationships.

Objection: "We already use [Competitor] for cost management."
Response: "That's common — a lot of our customers came from [Competitor] for cost visibility. Where they typically hit a wall is governance enforcement across AWS and Azure in the same workflow. Is that something your team is managing today?"

Landmine: Don't compare on pricing unprompted — their enterprise contracts often bundle governance features at no extra cost.

Step 2 · ~15 min

Objection-handling guide

Prompt — objection handling

Build an objection-handling guide for [product] targeting [ICP]. Top 5 objections: 1. [Objection 1] 2. [Objection 2] 3. [Objection 3] 4. [Objection 4] 5. [Objection 5] For each: - The underlying concern behind the objection - A response that acknowledges it before addressing it - A follow-up question to keep the conversation moving - One proof point Conversational tone — this is for sales reps, not a formal document.

Step 3 · ~30 min

Format for sales with Gamma

Paste the Claude output into Gamma to produce a clean, shareable one-pager or slide deck. Review and edit before sharing — AI formatting needs a pass to feel on-brand.
